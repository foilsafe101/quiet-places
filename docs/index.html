About Shhh

Shhh is a dynamic, web-based map that locates places where little or no anthropogenic sound can be heard — no airplanes, motor vehicles, trains, explosions, machines, factories, wind turbines, human settlements, humans, or pets. In practical terms, that means no nearby roads or active railroad tracks, and no aircraft flying within ~20 miles.

Users can pan, zoom, and select various layers. Flights show up as red lines and are updated periodically with new data from OpenSky. Quiet zones show up in blue.
